# Homepage Shell Art Direction

Generated from the repo state on `2026-04-17`.

## Decision

- Keep the homepage/menu shell as the current visual reference surface.
- Do not redesign the homepage as part of the current cleanup pass.
- Treat the portal / void-like treatment around the logo as optional shell language, not as a mandatory pattern for every surface.

## Context

- The homepage at `/` is already the strongest visual surface in the repo.
- Earlier direction-setting for the cleanup chose the immersive-shell track, but the homepage itself was explicitly treated as already “in order”.
- The cleanup goal is therefore to harmonize the rest of the system around the homepage language, not to reopen the homepage first.

## What Stays Canonical

- `MainMenu` remains the visual anchor for:
  - framed composition
  - 3D rotating logo
  - layered media background
  - gothic button language

## What Does Not Become Mandatory

- A portal / void treatment around the logo is allowed as an enhancement on the homepage.
- It is not a required design-system primitive for `/shop`, PDP, `/collections`, or future lookbook/photo surfaces.
- Other surfaces should borrow the shell’s material language and atmosphere without copying the homepage composition literally.

## Practical Direction

- Preserve homepage/menu visual identity during cleanup.
- Use it as a reference for:
  - frame language
  - background-media treatment
  - button treatment
  - density and mood
- Do not block other refactors on unanswered portal-art-direction questions.

## Result

The homepage is a stable visual benchmark. The rest of the cleanup should aim for harmony with it, while staying free to use simpler compositions where the product surface needs clarity over spectacle.
